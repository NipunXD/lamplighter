# 5-minute pitch — shot list

Record at 1440×900 with the app on the left and the terminal/journal visible. Talk over it; no slides needed except maybe a title card.

| time | on screen | say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Title card → the town at dusk, all lanterns dark | "Saanjh & Co. is a small tea and candle studio on Razorpay. Every week money slips away: a UPI request times out, a card hits its limit, an AutoPay mandate gets paused, a café doesn't pay its invoice. Each of these houses is a customer. Each dark lantern is money that was almost theirs. Lamplighter is the agent that goes and relights them — inside the rules." |
| 0:25–1:15 | Press **Light the lamps**. Lanterns start flickering. Click a house: drawer shows Razorpay error, diagnosis with confidence and source, then the candidate list with ✓/✗ and failed rules | "Step one is diagnosis. Razorpay's error object is structured, but the interesting part is often the raw bank string. Rules handle the clear cases; a local Qwen model reads strings like `U30: DEBIT HAS BEEN FAILED` and classifies them. Then the policy engine enumerates every action and runs every rule. Notice the silent retry is blocked here — hard decline, never retry — and the voice call is blocked because the amount is too small for a call. The model only chooses among what's already allowed." |
| 1:15–2:00 | Journal: show an LLM plan line with reasoning, then a Hinglish message, then the Razorpay `order_created` line | "The model explains its pick in one line. It writes the message in the customer's language — Hinglish in Roman script — and a validator checks the amount, the merchant name, the opt-out line, and bans threatening words. Every outreach creates a real Razorpay test-mode Order and links to a checkout page." |
| 2:00–2:40 | Open a pay page from the drawer, pay with a test method, come back: the lantern flares, journal shows `signature_verified`, `payment_captured`, ledger "paid via Razorpay Checkout" ticks up | "This is the loop closing for real: Razorpay Checkout, signature verified with HMAC on the server, payment captured, lantern lit." |
| 2:40–3:20 | Let the clock run to 21:00: sky goes dark, lamplighter sits at the well, ribbon says quiet hours; journal shows `action_deferred … 09:00 IST`. Click "Customer replies STOP" on a house → journal shows the STOP and the close | "Compliance is part of the world, not a footnote. Nine at night, the lamplighter stops. A customer replying STOP ends contact immediately, including any retry that was already scheduled. Three knocks maximum, no repeats, DND respected, and every extra knock costs goodwill in the expected-value math." |
| 3:20–4:10 | Ledger + `docs/METRICS.md`: recovered vs at-risk, baseline comparison row, complaints, violations, diagnosis accuracy, LLM fallbacks | "Numbers on 120 cases over a simulated week: recovered X of Y at risk against Z for a naive retry cron, with fewer touches, fewer complaints, and zero policy violations versus N. Diagnosis accuracy went from A% with rules alone to B% with the model. The customers are simulated with hidden state the agent never reads, and I say so in the report." |
| 4:10–4:45 | Terminal: `pnpm test`, `pnpm batch --chaos 0.2` scrolling with `api_retry` / `llm_fallback` lines; `curl …/verify` returning `ok: true` | "Failure is exercised, not promised. Chaos injection makes Razorpay and the model fail on demand; retries, backoff, circuit breaker, template fallbacks, and the hash-chained audit log keep the run honest." |
| 4:45–5:00 | The town, most lanterns lit, one blue flag | "Escalations go to humans with a packet of what was tried. That's Lamplighter: bounded, explainable, measured. Thank you." |

Tips: keep the LLM on but batch size 40 for the live run so it finishes inside the recording; have a 120-case report pre-generated. Use `?speed` controls; pause when you talk over the drawer.
